## About SINSAY

SINSAY connects tourists with the accredited dive sites and dive resorts of Mabini, Batangas. It has two experiences:

- **Tourist (diver) app** — discover dive sites and resorts, build your digital **Eco-Dive ID**, plan trips with an AI assistant, and manage your profile.
- **Operator app** — for dive establishments. Track divers and pass credits, create **Electronic Dive Manifests**, and buy dive pass credits.

Your role is detected automatically after login. Approved operators land on the Operator app; everyone else lands on the Tourist app. Operators can switch to the tourist view at any time (see [Operator Profile](#operator-profile)).

## Get Started

### Install the App

SINSAY is a Progressive Web App (PWA) and runs in your browser like a normal app.

1. Open SINSAY in your mobile browser.
2. Go to **Profile → Install SINSAY App**.
3. Confirm the install prompt. The label changes to **App Installed** once done.

### Create an Account / Log In

From the welcome screen, choose **Create an Account** or **Log In**.

**Signing up** requires:

| Field | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Full Name | Required. Duplicate names are not allowed. |
| Email | Valid email address. Duplicate emails are not allowed. |
| Password | At least 8 characters, 1 capital letter, and 1 number. |

1. Enter your full name, email, and password. The checklist shows your progress (0/3 to 3/3 met).
2. Tap **Register**.
3. If email confirmation is enabled, check your inbox, confirm your account, then log in.

**Logging in** uses your email and password. You can also use **Continue with Google**.

> Contact numbers everywhere in the app use the Philippine format **+63 9XXXXXXXXX** (the `+63` prefix is fixed; enter 10 digits starting with 9).

### Forgot Password

1. On the **Log In** screen, tap **Forgot Password?**.
2. Enter your email and send the reset link.
3. Open the link in your email.
4. Enter a new password (8+ characters, 1 uppercase letter, 1 number) and confirm it.
5. Tap **Save New Password**, then return to **Log In**.

### Onboarding & Guided Tour

New accounts see three short eco-diving slides:

1. **Respect Marine Life** — look, don't touch; no-touch/no-take policy for corals and marine life.
2. **Protect the Ocean** — keep the Verde Island Passage clean; no single-use plastics; reef-safe sunscreen only.
3. **Maintain Control** — master neutral buoyancy; never touch or rest on reefs.

Tap **Next** to advance, **Skip** on the first two slides, or **Done** on the last. Swipe gestures also work.

After onboarding, a one-time **spotlight tour** highlights the main parts of the app (5 steps for tourists, 4 for operators). You can replay it anytime from **Profile → App Navigation Tutorial**.

## Tourist App

### Tourist Navigation Overview

The bottom navigation has four tabs:

| Tab | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Discovery hub — search, announcements, popular sites, verified resorts |
| **Map** | Interactive map of dive sites and protected areas |
| **Eco-Dive ID** | Your digital diver pass, profile progress, and dive history |
| **Profile** | Account, favorites, settings, and support |

The **AI Dive Assistant** is reached through the floating assistant button (or the "Ask AI" entry), not a tab. Notifications open from the bell icon on Home and the AI screens.

### Home

Your landing page after login.

- **Greeting** with your first name, plus a **notification bell** (badge shows unread count) and your **avatar** (opens Profile).
- **Search bar** — type at least 2 characters to search dive sites and resorts by name, location, or type. Tap a result to open it.
- **Category filters** — All, Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced. Filters the Popular Dive Sites rail by difficulty.
- **Announcements carousel** — tap a banner to read the full advisory from the Tourism Office.
- **Popular Dive Sites** — swipe the cards; tap a card for details, or the heart to save it as a favorite. **See all** opens the full list.
- **Verified Resorts** — accredited establishments only. Tap a card for details. **See all** opens the directory.
- **Floating AI assistant button** — opens the Dive Assistant.

### Map

An interactive map of Mabini's dive sites, Marine Protected Area (MPA) zones, and the territorial boundary line.

- **Pins** are color-coded by difficulty:
  - Green — Beginner / Open Water
  - Amber — Intermediate / Rescue
  - Red — Advanced / Divemaster / Instructor
- **Controls**: zoom in, zoom out, locate me, and reset view.
- **Tap a pin** to open a slide-up card with the site photo, name, location, depth, features, difficulty, marine life, eco-alert rules, and a "Did You Know" note.
- **Heart icon** saves the site to your favorites.
- **List** button switches to the Dive Sites list.
- Tap the map background to close the site card.

### Dive Sites

Open from **Home → Popular Dive Sites → See all**, or from the Map's **List** button.

- A hero carousel highlights featured sites.
- Browse **All Dive Destinations** as cards showing difficulty, depth, location, and description.
- Tap the **heart** on a card to favorite it.
- Tap a card or **Explore** to open the site detail.

**Dive Site Detail** shows the full overview, marine life, conservation rules, and a **View on Interactive Map** button that opens the Map focused on that site.

> Sites marked *Restricted* are hidden from tourists.

### Dive Resorts & Establishments

Open from **Home → Verified Resorts → See all**.

- Only **accredited** resorts are listed.
- Filter by **All**, **PCSSD Accredited**, or **DOT Accredited**.
- Tap a resort to view its profile: overview, accreditation badges, and **contacts & booking links**.

**Resort Detail** lists booking channels (Facebook/Messenger, Agoda, WhatsApp, Booking.com, Viber, TripAdvisor, Klook, Airbnb, Instagram, website) as buttons. Tapping one opens the external app or site. The phone number opens your dialer and the email opens your mail app.

> SINSAY does not process bookings or payments for resorts — you contact the resort directly.

### Eco-Dive ID

This is your digital diver pass and the most important screen in the tourist app.

**The ID card shows:**

- Your photo (tap to upload/change)
- Your **Eco-Dive ID number**
- A **status pill**: **Active**, **Expired**, **Ready**, or **Inactive**
- Your name, nationality, type of dive, emergency contact, and — for certified divers — certification level, date accredited, and renewal date

Tap the card to open a full-details view with an **Edit** button.

**Status meanings:**

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Inactive** | Profile not yet complete (or still being set up). |
| **Ready** | Profile is complete and you are waiting to be added to a dive manifest. |
| **Active** | A dive operator has submitted you on a verified manifest. Your pass is live. |
| **Expired** | Your pass has lapsed and needs renewal. |

> Activation is never done manually by you. An operator activates your ID by adding you to a dive manifest. It also refreshes automatically when the status changes.

**Dive history** appears below the card as a timeline of your logged dives. Tap **View Recap** on any dive to open a shareable story-style recap image, then **Save Image**.

When your profile is incomplete, the card shows a progress bar and a **Complete Profile** button.

### Complete Your Dive Profile

Open from **Eco-Dive ID → Complete Profile** (or **Profile → Dive Pass & Certification**).

**Step 1 — Personal Info:**

- Full Name
- Nationality
- Emergency contact name and Philippine phone number

**Step 2 — Dive Credentials:**

- **Type of Dive**: Fun Dive / Intro Dive / Guest, or Dive Instructor / Master.
- **Instructor / Master** path also requires:
  - Certification proof (upload)
  - Date accredited
  - Renewal date (renewal proof optional)
- **Fun / Intro / Guest** needs no certification.
- Read and check the **Data Privacy Act consent** box.

Tap **Continue**, then **Complete Profile**. You return to your Eco-Dive ID.

### AI Dive Assistant

Open the floating assistant button, or the AI entry from the app.

**Landing screen** has two options:

- **Ask AI Dive Questions** — open the chat
- **Generate Dive Plan** — open the trip planner
- **Starter pills** jump into chat with a suggested question

**Chat**

- Ask about dive rules, eco-guidelines, marine conservation, fees, and Mabini tourism information.
- Replies use formatted text with a typing indicator.
- If a message fails, tap **Retry** on that message.
- The **Plan Your Trip** pill opens the planner.
- Chat history is kept only for the current session and is not saved.

**Trip Planner**

1. Enter **Budget (PHP)**, **Group Size**, **Activities**, and **Length of Stay**.
2. Tap **Generate Trip Plan**.
3. The result includes an overview, a day-by-day schedule (morning/afternoon/evening), suggested dive sites with **View on Map**, an eco reminder, and a budget breakdown.
4. Use **Copy**, **Share**, **Regenerate**, **Edit inputs**, or **Start over**.
5. Tap **Save Itinerary** to keep it. Saved plans appear under **Profile → Favorites**. If you're signed in, plans are saved to your account; otherwise they stay on the device.

> The assistant is informational only. It does not activate your Eco-Dive ID, verify identity, or process payments.

### Profile & Settings

Your account hub.

**Account section**

- **My Account** — edit your profile
- **Favorites** — favorite dive sites and saved AI itineraries (filters: All, Dive Sites, Itineraries)
- **Dive Pass & Certification** — open the dive profile wizard
- **Language** — switch English / Filipino instantly
- **Apply as Operator** — begin an operator application (shows Pending/Rejected/Approved once submitted; pending applications cannot resubmit)
- **Log Out** — confirm to end your session

**More section**

- **App Navigation Tutorial** — replay the guided tour
- **Install SINSAY App** — install the PWA
- **Help & Support** — contact info, activation help, AI limits, report an issue
- **FAQ** — expandable answers
- **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Service**

**Edit Profile** lets you change your photo, full name, contact number, email, age, and sex. Certified divers and instructors can also add PCSSD Accreditation Number and Permit to Operate Number. Changing your email sends a confirmation link to the new address.

**Apply as Operator** is a 3-step form:

1. **Resort** — resort name (suggestions from accredited unclaimed listings) and location
2. **Contact** — role (Owner/Proprietor or Staff), contact number, optional Facebook and website
3. **Documents** — **Business Permit** (required), PCSSD and DOT accreditation (optional), and a confirmation checkbox

Submit to send your application for Tourism Office review. You'll see a pending screen; if rejected, you can re-apply.

### Notifications

Tap the **bell icon** to open notifications. Unread items are highlighted with a dot; opening the list marks them read.

Notification types include Eco-Dive ID activation, dive plan ready, operator application approved/rejected, pass purchase verified, manifest submitted, and new announcements. Tapping a notification takes you to the relevant screen; announcement notifications open the full advisory.

## Operator App

### Operator Navigation Overview

The bottom navigation has four tabs:

| Tab | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Live stats, quick actions, and recent manifests |
| **Manifests** | All manifests, drafts, and downloadable snapshots |
| **Buy Pass** | Purchase dive pass credits via GCash |
| **Profile** | Resort profile, account, and settings |

### Dashboard

Your operator home.

- **Header** — resort name greeting, notification bell, and avatar (opens Profile).
- **Pass warning banner** — appears when you have no remaining passes, with a shortcut to buy more.
- **KPI cards:**
  - **Today's Divers** — today's diver count, with the change versus yesterday
  - **Remaining Passes** — credits left versus purchased
  - **Manifest Sent** — manifests created this week
- **Quick actions** — **Create Dive Manifest** and **Buy Dive Credits**
- **Recent Manifests** — latest five; tap **See all** to open the Manifests tab

### Create a Dive Manifest

Open from **Dashboard → Create Dive Manifest** or **Manifests → New Manifest**.

Boat dives use **4 steps**; shore dives use **3 steps**. A draft is saved automatically on your device once you add a diver.

**Step 1 — Dive Details**

- **Dive Date** — today or a future date
- **Dive Type** — Scuba Diving or Freediving
- **Mode of Diving** — Shore Entry or Boat Entry
- **Dive Location(s)** — 1 to 5 sites, searchable
- **Diving Difficulty** — Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or All Levels

**Step 2 — Boat & Crew** (boat entries only)

- Boat name, captain name, boat contact number
- At least one crew member — **crew are fee-exempt**

**Divers step**

- **Add Diver** opens a search:
  - Search registered tourists by name (2+ characters) or exact Eco-Dive ID (6+ characters). Selecting a result fills their ID, nationality, and dive level.
  - Or add a **walk-in** with first name, last name, nationality, and dive level.
- Divers already covered by an annual pass show **Annual**; use the **Use annual** toggle for others with available annual slots.
- **Boat Capacity** (boat entries) caps the roster size.

**Safety & Review**

- **Duty of Care** — assign 1 to 5 dive masters/instructors and add optional notes
- Review the full manifest summary
- See how many passes will be deducted and what remains
- Tap **Submit Manifest**

After submission you'll see a confirmation with the manifest snapshot, a **Download Manifesto** image, **Share**, remaining pass credits, and buttons to return to the Dashboard or view all manifests.

> **Pass consumption:** walk-ins and registered divers each use a pass, except divers covered by an annual pass. If you don't have enough passes, submit is blocked until you buy more.

### Manifests

Browse and manage all manifests.

- **Tabs** — **Upcoming**, **Completed**, and **Drafts**, each with a count.
- **Search** by boat name or location (drafts search by title).
- **Status pills** are based on the dive date:
  - **Active** — dive is today
  - **Upcoming** — dive is in the future
  - **Completed** — dive date has passed
- **Tap a manifest** to open the official snapshot preview. You can zoom, fit to width, **Download** it as an image, or **Share** it. Diver names, Eco-Dive IDs, nationality, and certification appear on the snapshot; walk-ins are labeled.
- **Drafts** — tap **Continue Draft** to resume a saved manifest, or the trash icon to delete it.

### Buy Dive Pass Credits

A 3-step purchase flow.

**Step 1 — Select Pass**

- See your current one-day pass balance.
- Choose a **quantity** with the steppers, number input, or presets (`+5`, `+10`, `+20`, `+50`). Valid range is 1 to 100.
- Review the price per pass and the total.
- Tap **Proceed to Pay**.
- **Recent Transactions** shows your latest purchases with status (Approved, Under Review, Rejected).

**Step 2 — Pay via GCash**

- Scan the merchant QR code (or the demo QR if no official QR is configured).
- Copy the account name and number — tap to copy.
- Pay the exact total in GCash, then tap **Proceed to Upload Receipt**.

**Step 3 — Upload Receipt**

1. Upload your GCash receipt (JPG, PNG, or PDF, up to 5 MB).
2. Enter the **13-digit GCash reference number**.
3. Tap **Submit Receipt for Verification**.

Your purchase shows **Under Review** until the Tourism Office verifies it. Once approved, the pass credits are added automatically. You'll see a verification timeline and can return to **Buy Pass** from the confirmation screen.

### Operator Profile

Your resort and account hub.

- **Identity card** — resort name, Verified badge, location, and PCSSD/DOT accreditation chips
- **Edit resort profile** — update your public resort details (see below)
- **About Resort** — description
- **Contact Links** — phone, email, website, and Facebook (open externally)
- **Switch to tourist** — preview the tourist app using your account
- **Account & Support** — signed-in details, **My Account**, **Language**, **Log Out**, **App Navigation Tutorial**, **Install app**, **Help & Support**, and **FAQ**

**Edit Resort Profile** has three tabs:

- **Resort** — resort name, location, description, phone, email, website, Facebook, and booking channel links (platform plus URL, add/remove)
- **Photos** — up to 3 resort photos (the first is the cover) plus your account avatar
- **Account** — full name, personal phone, and email (email changes require inbox confirmation)

Tap **Save changes** to apply updates.

## Quick Reference

### Status Glossary

**Eco-Dive ID**

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Inactive | Profile incomplete or being set up. |
| Ready | Profile complete; waiting to be added to a manifest. |
| Active | Included on a verified dive manifest; pass is live. |
| Expired | Pass has lapsed and needs renewal. |

**Manifest**

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Active | Dive date is today. |
| Upcoming | Dive date is in the future. |
| Completed | Dive date has passed. |
| Draft | Saved on this device; not yet submitted. |

**Pass Purchase**

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Under Review | Receipt submitted; awaiting Tourism Office verification. |
| Approved | Verified; credits added to your account. |
| Rejected | Not verified; check the reference and receipt and try again. |

### Troubleshooting

- **"Insufficient passes" when submitting a manifest** — Buy more pass credits, or assign annual passes to eligible divers.
- **My pass purchase is still "Under Review"** — The Tourism Office verifies payments manually, usually within 24–48 hours. Credits appear after approval.
- **"This GCash reference number was already submitted"** — Each reference can be used once. Check your receipt or contact support.
- **My Eco-Dive ID stays "Ready"** — Activation happens when an operator adds you to a submitted manifest. Make sure your profile is complete and ask your dive resort to include you.
- **Resort changes aren't showing** — Tap **Save changes** in Edit Resort Profile; the first uploaded photo becomes the cover.
- **App feels out of date offline** — SINSAY caches data for offline use, but fresh content (announcements, balances, statuses) needs a connection.
- **Can't log in** — Use **Forgot Password** to reset, or confirm your email if you just registered. Duplicate names and emails are not allowed.

For additional help, open **Profile → Help & Support**, or contact the Mabini Tourism Office.
